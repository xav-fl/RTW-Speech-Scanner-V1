From April we will raise the apprenticeship levy transfer cap to 50 per cent. Larger employers will be able to fund training across their supply chains, and we expect this to lift apprenticeship completions in small firms by around a fifth within three years.

We are also committing to a real-terms floor on per-pupil funding for the remainder of this Parliament. Stability in school budgets is what allows head teachers to plan staffing over more than a single year.
