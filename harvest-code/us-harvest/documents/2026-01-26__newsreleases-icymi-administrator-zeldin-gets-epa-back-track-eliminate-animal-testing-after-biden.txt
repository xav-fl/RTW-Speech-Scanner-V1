WASHINGTON – On January 22, 2026, U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) Administrator Lee Zeldin announced that the agency is getting back on track to end mammalian animal testing and reviving the ambitious goal, first set during the Trump Administration, to eliminate the practice by 2035. The Biden Administration canceled the agency’s animal testing phase-out deadlines, slowing innovation on next-generation testing alternatives that spare animals and strengthen science. Now, EPA is charging ahead, prioritizing the development and rollout of high-quality methods that replace testing on vertebrate mammals such as rabbits, mice, rats, and dogs, advancing EPA’s Make America Healthy Again agenda and the gold standard of scientific excellence.
