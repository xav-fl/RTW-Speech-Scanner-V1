Nearly 70% of ICE arrests are of illegal aliens charged or convicted of a crime in the U.S.

said Acting Assistant Secretary Lauren Bis.

> Under President Trump and Secretary Mullin, ICE will continue removing these public safety threats from our communities. America will be safe again.
