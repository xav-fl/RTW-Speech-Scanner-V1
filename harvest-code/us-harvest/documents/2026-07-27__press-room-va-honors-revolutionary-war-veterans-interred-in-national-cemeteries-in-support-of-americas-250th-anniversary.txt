WASHINGTON — The Department of Veterans Affairs this weekend will honor 49 Veterans of the Revolutionary War who are interred or memorialized in VA national cemeteries or VA-administered soldiers lots in private cemeteries, as part of the yearlong effort to commemorate America’s 250th anniversary.

While Declaration of Independence was adopted by the Continental Congress July 4, 1776, most delegates signed it on Aug. 2, 1776. To mark that event, ceremonies will commence at 10 a.m. local time, Aug. 2, 2026, at 16 sites across the country:

Bath National Cemetery, New York

Baton Rouge National Cemetery, Louisiana

Beverly National Cemetery, New Jersey

Congressional Cemetery Government Lots, District of Columbia

Crown Hill National Cemetery, Indiana

Florida National Cemetery, Florida

Fort Bliss National Cemetery, Texas

Fort Winnebago Cemetery Soldiers’ Lot, Wisconsin

Gerald B H Solomon Saratoga National Cemetery, New York

Great Lakes National Cemetery, Michigan

Indiantown Gap National Cemetery, Pennsylvania

Jefferson Barracks National Cemetery, Missouri

Marietta National Cemetery, Georgia

Mountain Home National Cemetery, Tennessee

Springfield National Cemetery, Missouri

West Virginia National Cemetery, West Virginia

> Service, sacrifice and patriotism are qualities displayed by our Veterans going back to the very founding of our nation,

said VA Under Secretary for Memorial Affairs Sam Brown.

> Two hundred and fifty years later, we remain inspired by those who first took up arms in defense of liberty, and who, like the signers of the Declaration of Independence, risked their lives, their fortunes and their sacred honor to establish the United States of America.

NCA provides burial and memorial benefits to eligible Veterans, their spouses, and their dependents — at no cost to the family. This includes a gravesite in any of VA’s national cemeteries with available space, opening and closing of the grave, perpetual care, a government headstone, marker updated this link or medallion, updated this link a burial flag and a Presidential Memorial Certificate. Some Veterans may also be eligible for burial allowances.
