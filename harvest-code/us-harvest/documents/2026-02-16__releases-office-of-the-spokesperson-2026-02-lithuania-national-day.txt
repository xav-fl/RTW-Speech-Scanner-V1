On behalf of the United States of America, I extend my congratulations to the people of Lithuania as you mark your 108th Independence Day.

Lithuania is a trusted partner and a model Ally. Its sustained defense investments and serious approach to burden-sharing strengthen NATO and reinforce security on the Alliance’s eastern flank.

Lithuania’s leadership reflects a clear commitment to national sovereignty, democratic self-determination, and responsible governance. We welcome Lithuania’s resolve in addressing shared challenges, and in supporting a free and open international system.

As the United States approaches its 250th anniversary through the Freedom 250 initiative, we value partnerships grounded in independence, free-market principles, and shared responsibility for collective defense. I extend my best wishes to the people of Lithuania for continued strength, security, and prosperity.
