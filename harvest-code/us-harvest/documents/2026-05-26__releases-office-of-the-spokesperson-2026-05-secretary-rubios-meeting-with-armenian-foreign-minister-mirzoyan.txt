The below is attributable to Spokesperson Tommy Pigott:

Secretary of State Marco Rubio met with Armenian Foreign Minister Ararat Mirzoyan in Yerevan. The Secretary and Foreign Minister discussed the peace process with Azerbaijan, the Trump Route for International Peace and Prosperity (TRIPP), and Armenia’s upcoming elections. The two leaders announced a bilateral framework agreement on TRIPP and signed a Strategic Partnership Charter and a Memorandum of Understanding on Critical Minerals. These documents deepen our bilateral relationship and advance the agreements made between the United States and Armenia during President Trump’s historic August 8, 2025 peace summit between Armenia and Azerbaijan.
