Under Secretary of State for Public Diplomacy Sarah B. Rogers will travel to Venice, Italy, on April 21-24, 2026. While there, she will engage with and address an audience of foreign government and private sector counterparts at the Privacy and Data Governance Symposium, an annual event that aims to foster expert-level international dialogue, cooperation, and information sharing. These opportunities will advance U.S. efforts to promote digital privacy protections and freedom of expression and advance American interests in the region.
