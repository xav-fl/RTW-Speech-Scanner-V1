Ms Victoria Billing OBE has been appointed His Majesty’s Ambassador to the Kingdom of Denmark in succession to Ms Joëlle Jenny, who will be transferring to another Diplomatic Service appointment. Ms Billing will take up her appointment in April 2026.

Full name: Victoria Elizabeth Billing

Year Role

2022 to 2025 FCDO, Director Europe (Mediterranean and Western Balkans)

2019 to 2022 Dakar, HM Ambassador to Senegal, Guinea Bissau and Cape Verde

2017 to 2019 Department for Exiting the European Union, Deputy Director Institutions and Member States

2017 FCO, Private Secretary to the Foreign Secretary

2015 to 2017 FCO, Deputy Head of Mediterranean Department, Europe Directorate

2008 to 2015 MATL then special unpaid leave

2008 Change unit, DFID

2006 to 2008 Jerusalem, Deputy Consul General

2002 to 2005 FCO, Head of ESDP section then deputy head of CFSPD, Europe Directorate

1999 to 2002 Yangon, Second Secretary Political

1998 to 1999 Full time language training (Burmese)

1997 to 1998 FCO Bosnia desk officer

1997 Joined FCO
