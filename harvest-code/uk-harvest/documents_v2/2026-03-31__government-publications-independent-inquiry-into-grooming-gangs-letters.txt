The appointment of the Chair and panellists of the Independent Inquiry into Grooming Gangs was announced on 9 December, alongside the publication of the draft terms of reference.

The correspondence published here for transparency covers:

Baroness Casey’s recommendation to the Prime Minister and Home Secretary

an exchange of letters between the Home Secretary and the inquiry panel

an exchange of letters between the Home Secretary and the leader of Oldham Council

an exchange of letters between the inquiry Chair and Baroness Casey

The final terms of reference and accompanying correspondence between the Home Secretary and Baroness Longfield were published on 31 March 2026.
