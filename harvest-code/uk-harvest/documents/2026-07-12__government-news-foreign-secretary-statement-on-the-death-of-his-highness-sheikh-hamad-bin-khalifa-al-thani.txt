His Highness Sheikh Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani was a respected leader who strengthened the longstanding Qatar-UK friendship.

My sincere condolences to His Highness Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, the Al Thani family and the people of Qatar at this time of mourning.
