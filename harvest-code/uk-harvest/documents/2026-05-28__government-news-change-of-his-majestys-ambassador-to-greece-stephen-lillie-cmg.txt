Mr Stephen Lillie CMG has been appointed His Majesty’s Ambassador to the Hellenic Republic, in succession to Mr Matthew James Lodge, who will be moving on secondment to another Whitehall Department.

Mr Lillie will take up his appointment during September 2026.
