Copy of the letter from the Minister of State for Local Government and Homelessness, Alison McGovern MP in response to the to the Woking Commissioners’ seventh report.

The Minister has noted the progress made by the council and has emphasised the importance of maintaining momentum in delivering its commercial programme, strengthening housing services, and ensuring capacity ahead of local government reorganisation.
