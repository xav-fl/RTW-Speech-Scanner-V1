The Animal Sentience Committee sent this letter to the EFRA committee on 18 December 2025.

It sets out their initial thoughts on the recently published policy paper ‘Replacing animals in science: A strategy to support the development, validation and uptake of alternative methods.’

You can read more about the work of the Animal Sentience Committee.
