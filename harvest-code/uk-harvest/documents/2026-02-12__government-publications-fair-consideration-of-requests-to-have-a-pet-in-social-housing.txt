TITLE: Fair consideration of requests to have a pet in social housing

DATE: 2026-02-12
GOVUK_TYPE: correspondence

---

This letter reminds social housing landlords of the importance in fairly considering tenants’ requests to keep pets and ensuring pet policies are clear and accessible. Social landlords are encouraged to share best practice with one another on managing requests to keep a pet and the letter outlines examples of existing practice.
