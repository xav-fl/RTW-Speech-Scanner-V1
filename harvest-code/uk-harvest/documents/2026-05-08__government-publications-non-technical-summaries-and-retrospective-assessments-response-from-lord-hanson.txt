Following the publication the Animals in Science Committee’s advice on non-technical summaries and retrospective assessments, Lord Hanson of Flint has written to Dr Sally Robinson to set out the Home Office response.

The Home Office has grouped the recommendations into two categories, those that can be responded to now, and those requiring further consideration alongside future NC3Rs recommendations.
