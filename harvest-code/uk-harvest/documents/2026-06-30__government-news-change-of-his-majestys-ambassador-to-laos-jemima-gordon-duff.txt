Ms Jemima Gordon-Duff has been appointed His Majesty’s Ambassador to the Lao People’s Democratic Republic in succession to Ms Melanie Barlow, who will be transferring to another Diplomatic Service appointment.  Ms Gordon-Duff will take up her appointment during August 2026.
