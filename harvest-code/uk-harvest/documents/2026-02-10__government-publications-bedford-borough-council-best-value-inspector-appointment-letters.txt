Copies of letters from James Blythe, Deputy Director, Local Government Stewardship, at the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government, to the Inspection team appointed to undertake an Inspection of Bedford Borough Council under section 10 of the Local Government Act 1999. The appointment letters sets out the scope of the inspection and the responsibilities of the Inspector and Assistant Inspectors.
