DCMS letter to the solicitors acting on behalf of Paramount, informing them that the Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport is minded to refer the anticipated acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery Inc. by Paramount Skydance Corporation for further investigation by Ofcom and the Competition and Markets Authority.
