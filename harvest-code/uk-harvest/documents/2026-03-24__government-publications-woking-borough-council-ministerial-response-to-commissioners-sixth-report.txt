Copy of the letter from the Minister of State for Local Government and Homelessness, Alison McGovern MP in response to the to the Woking Commissioners’ sixth report. The Minister has noted the progress made by the Council and has emphasised the importance of the council’s capacity to implement changes swiftly, especially in preparation for local government reorganisation.
