In response to the final report of the Office of Value for Money, reforms have been made to the spending control and accountability framework. These reforms reset the controls and approval limits governing expenditure, enabling quick, efficient decision making, while ensuring accountability is maintained. Accounting officers are reminded of their responsibilities regarding the use of public money.
