​A letter to Surrey councils’ chief executives, confirming that a direction has been issued relating to financial transactions as part of Surrey’s local government reorganisation.

The letter sets out the direction and accompanying guidance note.

The letter was issued by Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government officials.
